ℹ️ What the numbers mean

Ripeness — how far through their usual gap between orders a customer is. 100% means they're due, on past behaviour. It's a prompt to ring, not a forecast.

Route — the direction out of Harare, not which tarmac. A mine off the road still sits on the route the truck takes to reach it.

Trip cost — road km × the truck's rate, depot out and back. The badge on the map tells you whether it used real roads or fell back to a straight line. Real roads are close, but mine and farm tracks are often missing from the map, so treat it as a good guide rather than a quote.

Lost sale — they bought, just not from us. Logging it keeps their clock running so we ring them at the right time next round instead of writing them off as gone quiet. It adds no litres and doesn't touch their average order size.

History — cycles are built on roughly 6.5 months of orders. Enough to spot a rhythm. Not enough to see a season.
