Record a sale

—
For a sale that did happen but never made it into the app. It counts as a normal sale for volume and resets their clock — and is flagged as back-entered so you can see how often logging gets missed.
Commission follows the rep, so pick the person who actually made the sale.

Link a name

—
If reps type this customer's name differently in the app, their sales don't match up and the customer looks quiet. Enter exactly what the reps type and it will be linked to this customer from now on — and their past sales will match too.
Spelling matters, but capitals and extra spaces don't.
